About Our School

A welcoming home for joyful, foundational learning

Budbud Hindi F.P. School is a government primary school located in Sukdal village, Galsi-I block, Purba Bardhaman district, West Bengal. Since 1953 we have been committed to giving every child a strong, happy and confident start to their education.

We follow the West Bengal Board of Primary Education curriculum and offer schooling from Pre-Primary right through to Class 5, blending traditional values with modern digital teaching, activities and festivals that make learning a celebration.

  • ✔ Friendly, child-centred classrooms
  • ✔ Smart interactive digital boards
  • ✔ Mid-day meal & safe campus
  • ✔ Eco Club, reading & cultural events

Principal's Message

“Every child who walks through our gate deserves the very best start.”

It gives me immense pride to lead a school that has served our community since 1953. At Budbud Hindi F.P. School we believe a primary school should feel like a second home — safe, joyful and full of curiosity.

Alongside the West Bengal Board curriculum, we embrace digital teaching, environmental awareness and cultural activities so that our children grow into confident, kind and capable young people. We warmly welcome you to be a part of our school family.
